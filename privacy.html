Privacy Policy

Kodi App is used only by its owner, on the owner's own devices.

What data is accessed

After the owner signs in with their Google account, the app reads that account's YouTube data (subscriptions, playlists, watch history) through the YouTube Data API, only to show it in Kodi.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own devices. No data is sent to any other server, sold, or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
